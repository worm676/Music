Tips

  • Drag clips to move them in time or onto another track.
  • Drag a clip's edges to trim it.
  • Right-click a clip to duplicate, split or delete.
  • Drop audio files straight onto a track lane.
  • Hold Alt while dragging to ignore snap.

Describe your beat

Understands genres (trap, drill, boom bap, lo-fi, house, techno, R&B, pop, reggaeton, afrobeats, drum & bass, synthwave), moods (dark, happy, eerie, chill…), tempo ("140 bpm"), key ("in F# minor"), instruments (piano, rhodes, pads, bells, pluck, lead, arp) and "no drums / no melody".

Voice overlay

  1. Arm a track with its R button (the “Vocals” track is armed already).
  2. Click the ruler to place the playhead where you want to start.
  3. Press ● (or R). The beat plays while you record. Press again to stop.

Import a vocal take

Use each track's FX knob to add reverb to vocals, Pan to place them in the stereo field.
